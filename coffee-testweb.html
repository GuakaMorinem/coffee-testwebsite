About Coffee

A Drink With Centuries of History

Coffee has traveled from ancient forests to modern cafés, becoming one of the world's favorite beverages.

From Bean to Cup

Coffee comes from the seeds, commonly called beans, of berries produced by coffee plants. The two most commercially important species are Arabica and Robusta.

Coffee's history is traditionally traced to Ethiopia, where coffee plants are believed to have originated. From there, coffee cultivation and trade spread to Yemen and throughout the Middle East.

By the 17th century, coffee had reached Europe and coffeehouses began appearing in major cities. Today, coffee is enjoyed in countless forms, from a simple espresso to elaborate specialty drinks.

Explore Different Coffee Types

Each preparation method creates a different flavor, texture, and experience.

Espresso

🇮🇹 Italy

A concentrated coffee brewed by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure.

Strong Rich Intense

Cappuccino

🇮🇹 Italy

Traditionally made with espresso, steamed milk, and a layer of milk foam. Its modern form developed in Italy.

Creamy Foamy Balanced

Caffè Latte

🇮🇹 Italy

Espresso combined with plenty of steamed milk and a small amount of foam, creating a smooth and gentle drink.

Smooth Milky Mild

Americano

🇺🇸 United States / Italy

Espresso diluted with hot water. It provides a longer drink while retaining the characteristic flavor of espresso.

Bold Light Simple

Mocha

🇾🇪 Yemen / Modern Café Culture

A chocolate-flavored coffee drink generally made with espresso, steamed milk, and chocolate.

Chocolate Sweet Creamy

Cold Brew

🌎 Modern Coffee Culture

Coffee grounds steeped in cold or room-temperature water for many hours, producing a smooth and less acidic drink.

Cold Smooth Refreshing

French Press

🇫🇷 France

Coarsely ground coffee is steeped in hot water before a metal mesh plunger separates the grounds from the drink.

Full-bodied Rich Aromatic

Pour Over

🇩🇪 Germany / Global

Hot water is slowly poured over coffee grounds in a filter, allowing precise control over extraction and flavor.

Clean Bright Aromatic

Macchiato

🇮🇹 Italy

Espresso marked with a small amount of steamed or foamed milk. The name means "stained" or "marked" in Italian.

Strong Foamy Small
Timeline

The History of Coffee

A quick journey through the development and spread of coffee.

9th Century

Origins in Ethiopia

Coffee plants are believed to have originated in the highlands of Ethiopia. Stories about coffee's discovery include the famous legend of a goat herder named Kaldi.

15th Century

Yemen and Coffee Cultivation

Coffee cultivation and preparation became established in Yemen, where coffee became an important part of social and religious life.

16th Century

Coffee Spreads Across the Middle East

Coffeehouses became popular gathering places in cities across the Middle East, supporting conversation, commerce, and culture.

17th Century

Arrival in Europe

Coffee reached Europe and quickly became popular. Coffeehouses appeared in cities such as London, Paris, and Vienna.

18th Century

Global Cultivation

European colonial powers established coffee plantations in tropical regions around the world, expanding global production.

20th–21st Century

The Specialty Coffee Era

Espresso culture, specialty coffee, home brewing, latte art, single-origin beans, and modern brewing techniques have transformed contemporary coffee culture.
